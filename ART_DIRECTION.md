# Art direction and provenance

Five original illustrations generated with the built-in image generation tool for this project, 2026-10-03. PNG originals were converted to 768×1152 JPEGs at quality 84 for offline Android packaging. No semantic image edits or third-party stock assets were used.

Final art: `app/src/main/assets/art/{cover,table,jiho,hana,letter}.jpg`.

## Shared direction

Use case: illustration-story. Portrait 2:3. An original Korean-inspired emotional visual novel, Raining. Beautiful hand-painted anime with fine expressive linework, watercolor grain, cinematic muted petrol-blue rainy evening contrasted with amber light. Mature, restrained, intimate everyday drama. No typography, no logos, no watermarks. Subsequent illustrations use previous generated illustrations as continuity references.
